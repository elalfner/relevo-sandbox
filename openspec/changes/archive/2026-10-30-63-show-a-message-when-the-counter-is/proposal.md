## Why

The person using the app gets a small celebration when the counter reaches thirty-one.

## What Changes

- Show the text "Thirty-one!" under the counter value while the counter is exactly 31.
- Remove it as soon as the value changes.

## Capabilities

### New Capabilities
- `counter-thirty-one`: message shown under the counter when its value is 31.

### Modified Capabilities

## Impact

- `lib/main.dart` (counter screen); new widget test. No dependencies.
